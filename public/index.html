Team & devices

Everyone who helps , and every device the board is on.

People on this device

Each person keeps their own words, groups, and sync. Switching opens that person; the others keep everything they had.

When Pip opens on this device

A child's own device opens straight to their board. On a shared or therapy device, "Show the list" asks who's talking each time Pip starts.

You're on the team

You can edit everything on 's board. An owner invites and removes people, adds devices, manages the license, and can delete the board.

Team

People you invite join the team: they edit everything, on their own phone or laptop. Make owner lets them manage people and devices too. Remove locks them out.

Devices

A linked device sees and edits everything for this person. Removing one locks it out.

Backup & privacy

Keep 's words safe, and choose what leaves this device.

Recovery card

If this device is lost or reset, the card brings everything back. Print it and keep it somewhere safe. Without the card, a linked device, or a device backup, their words are gone for good.

Settings PIN

No PIN yet: Settings opens with one tap.

One PIN for everyone on this device. Pip never shows it, so a child watching can't learn it. Forgot it? Tap “Forgot the PIN?” and type “new pin”.

Share anonymous counts with Pip

Daily totals only: word counts, sentence lengths, speed. Never names, 's own words, sentences, or tap times.

The speaking voice

Word labels you type or add are sent to a voice provider (ElevenLabs) to speak them in the board's voice — including names like 's. Only the word text is sent: never an account, email, photo, or recording.
