Backup & privacy

Keep 's words safe, and choose what leaves this device.

Recovery card

If this device is lost or reset, the card brings everything back. Print it and keep it somewhere safe. Without the card, a linked device, or a device backup, a lost board is gone for good.

Settings PIN

One PIN for everyone on this device. Pip never shows it, so a child watching can't learn it. Forgot it? The license key or the recovery card resets it.
