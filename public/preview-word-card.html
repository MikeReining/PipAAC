Word card: one look, ordered by what parents do

You tap a word and want to do one of four things: hear it, change its picture, change its voice, or put a different word in its spot. The card should read top to bottom in that order, and every control should look like what it does. No links, no one-item menus, no labels that say nothing.

1 · A choice looks like a choicePictures and voices sit in a row. The one Mia sees or hears has the ink ring. Tap another to switch.
2 · Making a new one is dashed📷 Photo, 🎙 Record and + Group are all the same dashed shape at the end of their row.
3 · One real button"Swap for another word" is the only big button, because it changes the board.
4 · Rare things whisperHide or Remove is plain text at the bottom. No "…" menu, nothing red.

Two bugs found while tracing this

Your recording saves but doesn't play on the iPad. The iPad records in MP4 format. The app saves the file with no file type attached, and plays it back with no file type either (public/db.js savePhoto → loadPhotoURL → speech.js playClip). If Safari had clearly refused to play it, the app would have said the word in the device voice instead. You heard nothing, which means playback got stuck rather than failing outright. That fits Safari stalling on a file with no type.

Fix: work out the file type from the file's first few bytes when playing it back, and play the recording right after Stop (part of the redesign). Not proven yet. It only counts once you record on your iPad and hear it play back.

"More where" shows up twice on "down". There's one "More where" group for the 60-word board and another for the 30-word board, and the card lists both. It should only list groups on the board size Mia uses.
