Privacy

What we can see, and what we can't.

Pip is built so the things your family makes belong to your family's devices. This page says exactly what leaves them, and when.

What stays on the device.

  • The board. Every word, group, photo, and recording your family makes is stored on the device itself — it works with no internet at all.
  • Your names and people. The names and photos you add for family and friends live on the device.
  • Sentence history and progress. What your child has said, and the counts behind the progress report, are stored on the device — not on our servers.

What backup does.

When a grown-up turns on backup, every change is sealed on the device before it goes anywhere. It travels to our servers only as an encrypted envelope we cannot open, and it can only be read by the devices your family has linked — the iPad, a parent's phone, a therapist's laptop. If the iPad breaks, a linked device brings the whole board back. We still can't read any of it.

What the cloud features send — exactly.

Some features need a server to do their job. Each one sends the smallest thing it needs, only when you use it:

  • The natural sentence voice. When Pip reads a whole sentence aloud in its natural voice, that sentence's text goes to the voice provider to be recorded — once. The recording is kept so the same sentence never has to be made twice.
  • The sentence buttons (✨ ❓ ⏪ ⏩). Before a sentence is sent for grammar help, every name your family entered is swapped for a stand-in — "PERSON1" — so "Aunt Rosa fell" arrives as "PERSON1 fell". The real names never leave the device.
  • A voice for a word you typed. Adding a new word sends that single word — nothing else — to the voice provider so it can speak.
  • Drawn pictures. Asking Pip to draw a picture sends the word you are illustrating to the picture provider.

These features come with Pip Lifetime (and the free trial). Everything else — tapping words, hearing them, editing the board — needs no internet and sends nothing.

What we never do.

  • No listening. The microphone is used only when you choose to record a word.
  • No ads. No tracking. Not in the app, not on this website — which has no trackers and no cookies.
  • No selling. Nothing about your child is sold or shared for marketing. Helpers see the board only because your family invited them.
  • No card numbers. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe; we see a receipt, never a card.
