Questions parents ask us.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Free means free. Your child taps a word and Pip says it, for as long as they need it. You also get 20 words of your own, one helper, backup, and Spotlight. Every new board starts with a free week of everything, including natural sentences and the sentence buttons. After that, keeping those costs $49 once. That's the whole catch.

What does $49 get me?

A natural voice that reads the whole sentence, the sentence buttons, feeling faces, a choice of voices, a voice for words you type, the full progress dashboard and report, unlimited words of your own, every helper on the team, every device, and 300 drawings. Once. Forever.

Will you ever make it a subscription?

No. A voice is not rented. And the words your child already has keep speaking, whether you pay or not.

Will the buttons move around?

Never on their own. Suggestions live in a strip at the top; the board stays put. You decide if a word moves.

Can I make it look and sound like our family?

Yes. Put any word in any spot. Add your people with your photos and their real names. Record your own voice for any word. What you add beats our defaults everywhere.

Does it work on our iPad?

Yes. Pip runs on the iPad you already have, or any device with a modern browser. Tap Create your child's board and you're in. No download, no account to start, and once it's open it speaks every word without Wi-Fi.

Is $49 per child or per family?

Per child: the person who speaks. Helpers never buy their own license.

I'm an SLP. Do I pay?

Never. Set up as many clients as you like, and each family starts free.
