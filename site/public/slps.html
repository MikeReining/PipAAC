Progress reports

Your IEP progress report, in one tap.

IEP goals say "independently." Pip measures exactly that.

For every target word, Pip counts what your client said on their own separately from what they said with the glow of a Spotlight prompt. Pick the dates, tap Share, and the PDF is ready for the meeting.

  • Independent vs prompted, word by word, week by week.
  • The numbers goals are written against: different words, words per sentence, the longest sentence, and new words with the date each one first appeared.
  • Honest by design. Only the child's own taps count. When you or a parent model from a phone, it never inflates the numbers.
  • On your laptop. Daily totals reach every supporter on the team. Sentence history is stored on the family's devices — we can't read it. How privacy works →
