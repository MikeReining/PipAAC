About Pip

We built Pip because a voice should never be for sale.

And because the people who love a child know that child better than any company ever will.

Create your child's board — free →

What made us angry.

The price. $250 to $300 before a child can say a word. Families told to wait months for funding while their child has things to say.

The rent. Subscriptions that charge every month for a child to keep talking. A voice that stops when a payment does.

The template. One board for every child on earth, locked "for their own good," as if a company could know a child better than the child's own family.

So we built the opposite.

Our promises.

1

A voice is not rented.

Never a subscription to speak.

2

Words are never taken away.

A word your child has keeps speaking, paid or not.

3

Mom knows best.

Great defaults. Your call.

4

We never act on a guess.

Pip suggests. It never hides or clears your child's words.

5

Pip never listens.

No microphone unless you record a word.

6

Everything is ours.

Our pictures, our voices, our layout. We don't copy anyone's board.

Built on what the research says helps.

  • Presume competence. Every word is there from day one. No tests to pass first.
  • Core words. A few hundred words do most of the work in everyday talk, so they're always on the board.
  • Modeling. Children learn AAC the way they learn speech, by seeing it used. That's why Pip lets you model from your phone.
  • More than requests. Your child can protest, joke, comment, ask, and say how they feel, not just ask for snacks.
