"Customizable" usually means an evening of chores.

Adding one word, in most AAC apps
  1. Take the iPad away from your child.
  2. Find edit mode. Find an empty button.
  3. Type the label.
  4. Search the symbol library. No jack-in-the-box.
  5. Find the toy. Take a photo. Crop it.
  6. Pick a color. Save.
  7. Hand the iPad back. Hope he's still interested.

🎨

No photo needed.

Pip draws any word in the same calm style as the rest of the board, so it looks like it was always there. Photos are still there for the faces that need one: Grandma should look like Grandma.

💻

Never take the iPad away.

Add words from your laptop, your phone, any browser. Your child keeps talking while you work. Same rule as modeling: the iPad stays theirs.

🔗

One word, everywhere it lives.

Put Jack-in-the-box in Play and in My Words, and it's still one word. Change its picture or how it sounds once, and it changes everywhere.

For speech therapists

Fringe vocabulary in one paste.

Paste the list from the intake form or the family's email. Every word gets a picture in the board's style (people and pets wait for a photo), and the family's iPad has them the same day. You do it from your laptop, between sessions, not on the child's device during one.
