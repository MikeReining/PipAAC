Where Pip is different.

They rent it. You own it.

Proloquo is a subscription: $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year, for as long as your child needs to talk. Pip's every built-in word is free forever, and Pip Lifetime is $49 once.

Their board. Or yours.

Proloquo is built on the idea that every child should get the same board, and families shouldn't change it. Pip starts with great defaults, then hands you the keys: any word, any spot, your photos, your people, your voice.

Stable without the lock.

We agree words shouldn't jump around. In Pip, nothing moves on its own. But when you decide a word should move, it moves.

What you get with Pip.

  • Every built-in word, free forever. No trial, no countdown.
  • $49 once for unlimited words of your own and every helper.
  • Fully customizable: any word in any spot, your photos, your names, your recordings.
  • Stable: nothing on the board moves on its own.
  • Model from your phone: the word glows on your child's iPad.
  • Say it with feeling: happy, sad, or angry, for any sentence.
  • Runs on the iPad you own, in the browser. Nothing to download.
