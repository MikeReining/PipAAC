Money questions.

Is Pip really free? What's the catch?

Every built-in word speaks, free, forever. You also get 20 words of your own, your child's device plus one helper, backup, all voices, and feelings. If you want more, it's $49 once. That's the whole catch.

Will you ever make it a subscription?

No. A voice is not rented.

Is it $49 per child or per family?

Per child: the person who speaks. Every adult helper is free.

What happens to our words if we never pay?

Nothing. They keep speaking. Free has a limit on adding your own words, never on using the ones you have.

I'm an SLP. Do I pay?

Never. Set up as many clients as you like.

Can our school or district buy it?

Yes: license codes, 50% off 20 or more, purchase orders welcome.
