Money questions.

Is Pip really free? What's the catch?

Free means free. Your child taps a word and Pip says it, for as long as they need it. You also get 20 words of your own, one helper, backup, and Spotlight. Every new board starts with a free week of everything, including natural sentences and the sentence buttons. After that, keeping those costs $49 once. That's the whole catch.

Will you ever make it a subscription?

No. A voice is not rented.

Is it $49 per child or per family?

Per child: the person who speaks. Helpers never buy their own license.

What happens to our words if we never pay?

Nothing. They keep speaking. Free has a limit on adding your own words, never on using the ones you have.

I'm an SLP. Do I pay?

Never. Set up as many clients as you like.

Can our school or district buy it?

Yes: license codes, 50% off 10 or more, purchase orders welcome.
