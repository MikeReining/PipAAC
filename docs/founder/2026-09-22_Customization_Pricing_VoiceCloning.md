# Founder Intake: Customization, Ethical Pricing, and Voice Cloning

**Intake date:** 2026-09-22.
**Source:** Founder direction in session following competitive analysis of 440 Proloquo and Proloquo2Go App Store reviews.
**Intake workflow:** `docs/workflows/SSOT_Founder_Input_Workflow.md`.
Nothing in this file is the truth owner. Routed claims live in the docs named below.

---

## Founder intent

1. **Rejection of Forced Standardization:** Proloquo's refusal to allow deep customization is a failure. Every child and adult communicator is unique. Parents, educators, and communicators know their needs best. Pip AAC will support full customization while preserving spatial motor vectors.
2. **Ethical Pricing (The Anti-Subscription Law):** A human voice must never be held hostage behind a recurring \$10–\$15/month subscription. We will support a free core tier, an affordable one-time lifetime license, and Medicaid/state-funding friendly purchasing.
3. **Vocabulary Masking & Safety:** Parents and educators must be able to hide/mask words (e.g. "shut up", swear words, adult themes) without shifting adjacent cells or destroying motor planning.
4. **Accidental Deletion Prevention:** Strong, low-friction Parent Corner gating using PIN and native biometrics (Touch ID / Face ID via WebAuthn), backed by a non-destructive "Retire, Never Delete" restore/undo UI.
5. **Label-Only Mode for Adults & Literate Communicators:** Option to toggle off symbol art and display clean text labels with Fitzgerald color, eliminating visual infantilization for autistic adults, ALS, and aphasia communicators.
6. **Instant Voice Cloning (ElevenLabs / Mom's Voice):** Enable a parent or communicator to provide a 10–15 second audio sample to clone a voice (proven by Pip AAC's current founding SLP voice clone). The library generates cached offline clips so the child speaks with a familiar, loving voice rather than a generic synthetic voice.
7. **Cloudflare Backup & Multi-Device Sync:** Ensure that a shattered tablet never destroys years of customization.

---

## Product value

1. Parents and communicators feel respected rather than exploited: no predatory monthly subscription to speak.
2. Complete personalization: custom entities, word masking, label-only presentation, and mom/caregiver cloned voices.
3. Total peace of mind: zero data loss on broken devices and no accidental deletions by curious fingers.
