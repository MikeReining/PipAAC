## Founder intent

1. Spotlight, the upgrade highlight, prediction halos, and live partner
   modeling are one attention layer. Live partner modeling belongs in 013.
2. It must work from both devices: turn it on and off on the child's
   device, and customize it on the parent's or SLP's device.
3. It is not Edit mode. Selecting words to highlight is a different purpose
   from rearranging or editing them, even though the navigation looks
   alike. The user flow needs to make that clear.
4. Settle where it lives, how long it lasts, and whether it applies to the
   home board or a group.

## Agent recommendation, adopted in full

- A spotlight is a list of words, shown wherever they appear; inside a
  group the glow walks the route (Groups anchor → group → word).
- Shown on the child's device, controlled from either device. Turning on is
  an adult action; turning off is always one tap.
- Three lifetimes: live modeling (seconds), session (until ended, a timer,
  or midnight), saved list (until deleted).
- Live modeling is silent by default; the adult speaks aloud.
- Separate from Edit mode; they share only the board mirror component.
