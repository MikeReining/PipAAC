## 1. What an SLP needs

1. **Their client talking soon.** Today a child often waits months: an
   evaluation, a funding request, then a device.
2. **Their time back.** Setting up a device means having it in hand; it
   goes home with the child, and much of the setup happens unpaid at
   night.
3. **Practice at home.** Therapy is 30 minutes a week. The rest depends on
   parents modeling, and most parents do not know how.
4. **Looking good to the family.** SLPs recommend what makes them look
   competent and what the family can actually get.

Money is not on the list. The incentive is professional: less time,
better outcomes, a happier family. **No referral payments, ever**
(founder, 2026-09-23). We win by the best product.

## 2. The unlock

**The SLP starts a child on AAC in the first session, and the family has
it at home that night, free.** Pitch: **"No child waits for a voice."**

Competitors need \$250–\$300 up front or a funded device, so matching a
free first session costs them their revenue. The protection is the
business model, not a feature.

## 3. The SLP story (DECIDED 2026-09-23)

1. The SLP signs in on a laptop (free supporter account), taps **Add
   user**, and builds "Maya" in the session: starter words, family photos,
   bulk paste.
2. The SLP emails the QR card to the parents.
3. At home the parent scans it on Maya's iPad. The iPad becomes Maya's
   own device; the SLP stays her one free supporter
   (`docs/product/Pricing_And_Packaging.md` § 4.2).
4. The SLP keeps editing from the office; Maya's iPad has it that night.
5. Word 21, or a parent wanting it on their own phone, is the \$49
   moment: after the family has seen their child talk.

An SLP pays nothing to try Pip, onboards as many families as they like,
and each family tries it free. That makes outreach to SLPs simple: there
is nothing to buy before it works.

Also decided: a **free demo user** per supporter account for evaluations
(full features; cannot be moved or shared), and **one sign-in for the
whole caseload** (`docs/product/Sync_And_Web_Editing.md` § 12.4).
