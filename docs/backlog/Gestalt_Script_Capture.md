# Idea — Gestalt Script Capture & Mitigation Bridges

**Status:** Never decided (founder idea, 2026-09-30).

## 1. Clinical Context (Natural Language Acquisition)

In pediatric speech therapy, **Gestalt Language Processing (GLP)**—modeled through the
Natural Language Acquisition (NLA) framework (Blanc, 2012; Prizant et al.)—describes
communicators who acquire language in whole, unanalyzed "gestalts" or multi-word scripts
before breaking them down into flexible words and grammar.

Unlike Analytic Language Processors who start with single words (*"more"*, *"go"*) and
combine them into syntax (*"more milk"*), Gestalt learners start with whole chunks:
- Stage 1: Whole memorized gestalts / intonation scripts (*"Let's go check it out!"*, *"I gotta get out of here!"*).
- Stage 2: Mitigated gestalts (mixing and matching chunks: *"Let's go" + "to the park"*).
- Stage 3: Isolation of single words.
- Stages 4+: Generative grammar and novel syntax.

Traditional AAC applications force all users into Analytic single-word grids on day one,
leading many modern pediatric SLPs to push back against conventional AAC tools.

## 2. Why Generic Pre-Curated Lists Fail Gestalt Learners

Incumbent AAC apps attempt to support GLP by adding pre-written folders full of generic
phrases. This misses the clinical reality:
- Gestalt scripts are deeply personal, idiosyncratic, and tied to intense emotional
  meaning (favorite TV shows, family catchphrases, YouTube videos, bedtime songs).
- A child who uses *"To infinity and beyond!"* to mean *"I want to go outside"* will never
  find that in a generic hospital or AAC phrase list.

## 3. The Real Wedge: 2-Tap Script Capture & Mitigation Bridges

Instead of static phrase lists, Pip's relational engine can provide two high-leverage
capabilities:

1. **Two-Tap Script Capture (Caregiver & User):**
   - Tap `+ New Script` $\rightarrow$ speak or type the phrase $\rightarrow$ snap a photo or pick a character.
   - Saves directly to the child's `My Scripts` group in under 10 seconds.
   - Supports recording the exact vocal intonation (audio clip) which is critical for GLPs.
2. **The Mitigation Bridge (Stage 2/3 Scaffolding):**
   - When a phrase tile is tapped, it speaks the whole script naturally.
   - A subtle `Breakdown` pill allows an SLP or child to expand the script into the
     sentence bar as individual, recognizable words (`let's` · `go` · `outside`).
   - Visually connects the holistic gestalt to the individual core words on the main board.

## 4. Why This Sits in Backlog

uncontested market wedge for non-speaking adults with existing language models.

requires dedicated design validation with pediatric NLA-trained SLPs before scheduling
execution.
